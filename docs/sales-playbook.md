# Sales Playbook — Revenue Intelligence OS

## Primary ICP

Start narrow:
- Peruvian real-estate developers, brokers or commercial teams.
- Multiple sales advisors.
- Digital lead acquisition.
- A CRM or at least structured lead records.
- Management pain around follow-up, response time, attribution, advisor productivity, conversion or reporting.

Economic buyer hypotheses:
- Commercial Manager / Sales Director.
- General Manager in smaller firms.
- Marketing Manager when lead quality/attribution is the trigger.
- Operations/CRM owner as champion, not always final buyer.

## Problem statement

Do **not** lead with “AI dashboard”.

Lead with:

> “You are already paying to acquire leads. We identify where revenue leaks between lead, contact, appointment and sale, then turn that diagnosis into a weekly action queue for the commercial manager.”

## Discovery sequence

1. How many leads enter in a normal month?
2. Who sees them first and how quickly?
3. What happens if the advisor does not follow up?
4. Where does management currently see funnel conversion?
5. Which number do marketing and sales disagree about?
6. How frequently does management take action from CRM data?
7. What is one commercial problem that has existed for >3 months despite having reports?
8. What would improve economically if that problem disappeared?

## Qualification

High-priority signal:
- decision maker is involved,
- enough lead volume exists,
- pain is current,
- data exists,
- management can act on findings,
- and a pilot can be approved without enterprise procurement.

Disqualify / nurture:
- “interesting someday” with no current cost,
- no owner for the problem,
- no usable data and no willingness to create it,
- requires a multi-month custom build before value.

## First outreach structure

1. Relevant observation about their commercial context.
2. Specific leakage hypothesis.
3. One-sentence outcome.
4. Low-friction ask: 20-minute diagnostic.

Example logic:

> “I’m working on a revenue-intelligence workflow for real-estate sales teams. It focuses on the gap between paid leads and actual follow-up/conversion rather than adding another dashboard. I’m running a small founding cohort this week. If that is a current pain for [company], I can map the leakage with you in 20 minutes and show the control-room prototype.”

## Founding Pilot

**14 days — S/ 1,500 test price**

Deliverables:
- baseline funnel,
- leakage diagnosis,
- management scorecard,
- prioritized action queue,
- one automation or alert if justified,
- closing measurement review.

Boundary:
- one business unit / funnel,
- limited source integrations,
- no bespoke ERP rebuild,
- no ML until the baseline and action loop are trustworthy.

## Closing question

> “If the diagnostic identifies a leakage worth materially more than S/1,500 and we can implement the management loop in 14 days, what would prevent you from running the pilot?”

Record the answer verbatim.
