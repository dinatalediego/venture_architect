# 7-Day Company Launch

**Infrastructure checkpoint (2026-09-14): complete.** The public MVP, Supabase evidence path, GitHub control tower, CI and production smoke tests are live. From this point the scarce resource is customer evidence.

The founder gives this venture seven days of deliberate manual work. The goal is not to finish software. The goal is to create **external pull**.

## Day 1 — Positioning + founder outreach

**Outcome:** the product surface works and at least 20 targeted accounts enter the founder-selling workflow.

Manual work:
- Work Tier A first from `data/prospects.csv`.
- Identify a named commercial/marketing/general-management decision maker where possible.
- Send 10–15 highly personalized messages and make direct calls where appropriate.
- Ask for diagnosis conversations, not “feedback on my app”.
- Record objections verbatim.

Build ceiling: P0/P1 fixes only unless a live qualified opportunity is blocked.

## Day 2 — Problem evidence

**Outcome:** at least 3 live customer conversations or explicit objections.

Manual work:
- Continue targeted outreach.
- Run problem interviews where possible.
- Record exact language customers use for pain: response delay, lost leads, follow-up, attribution, manager visibility, advisor productivity, conversion, duplicated CRM work.
- Update landing copy only if customer language provides better evidence.

Decision question: **which pain is urgent enough to pay to solve this quarter?**

## Day 3 — Demo against real workflow

**Outcome:** prospects react to a concrete before/after workflow.

- Demonstrate the synthetic Revenue Intelligence control room.
- Ask prospect to describe their actual funnel and current weekly management ritual.
- Offer a free “Revenue Leakage Snapshot” using a small export or anonymized counts.
- Follow up every prior engagement.

Do not request sensitive production access to prove the concept. A spreadsheet/export is sufficient.

## Day 4 — Offer test

**Outcome:** test willingness to progress, not merely interest.

Present the Founding Pilot:
- 14 days.
- S/ 1,500 test price.
- One funnel integrated or manually loaded.
- Leakage map.
- Management scorecard.
- Prioritized action queue.
- Closing review with measured baseline and next-step recommendation.

Ask directly:

> “If we can deliver this scope in 14 days, is S/1,500 inside the range you could approve?”

Track yes / no / why.

## Day 5 — Deliver one sample diagnostic

**Outcome:** convert advice into visible customer value.

For the warmest account:
- Build a manual diagnostic from a limited export or their described numbers.
- Quantify one leakage point.
- Recommend 1–3 actions.
- Show the recurring version of the product.

This manual diagnostic is the seed of the future software workflow.

## Day 6 — Commercial concentration

**Outcome:** move the best opportunities to meetings/proposals.

- Call every prospect who engaged but did not book.
- Drop low-intent prospects from active attention.
- Send concise proposal to qualified accounts.
- Ask for introduction/referral when the prospect is not the buyer.
- Spend at least twice as much time selling as building unless a live deal is blocked by missing product capability.

## Day 7 — Close the learning loop

**Outcome:** a factual Day-8 decision packet.

- Final follow-ups.
- Update all metrics.
- Categorize objections.
- Rank the riskiest remaining assumption.
- Complete the committee review.
- Decide what to stop doing.

## Day 8 — Gate

Continue aggressively if:
- >=3 qualified B2B positive conversations / inbound leads, and
- >=1 meeting booked, proposal requested, or paid pilot.

Strong continue signal:
- a paid pilot, or
- >=2 meetings with decision makers, or
- repeated pain in the same workflow from >=5 prospects.

Pivot the offer/segment/channel if:
- sufficient targeted outreach happened but almost nobody progressed,
- customer language contradicts the offer,
- or interest exists but urgency/willingness to pay is weak.

Do not “pivot” into more software.
