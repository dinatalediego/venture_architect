## Evidence capture after every contact

Record:
1. response/no response;
2. exact objection or pain language;
3. urgency;
4. next observable action;
5. whether the hypothesized pain was confirmed, contradicted or unresolved.

A compliment is weak evidence.

A meeting, data sample, introduction, proposal request or payment is stronger evidence.
