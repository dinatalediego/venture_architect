# Day 1 — Decision-maker Outreach Brief

This document reduces research time. It does **not** claim these companies have the hypothesized problem.

Rule:

> Public context → specific question → short diagnostic ask.

Do not tell a prospect they have a leakage you have not observed.

## Evidence capture after every contact

Record:
1. response/no response;
2. exact objection or pain language;
3. urgency;
4. next observable action;
5. whether the hypothesized pain was confirmed, contradicted or unresolved.

A “nice idea” is not validation.

A meeting, data sample, introduction, proposal request or payment is stronger evidence.
