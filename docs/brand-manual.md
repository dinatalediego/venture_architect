# Venture Architect — Brand Manual v0.1

## Brand architecture

**Master brand:** Venture Architect  
**Initial product:** Revenue Intelligence OS  
**Commercial motion:** Founder-led diagnostic → Founding Pilot → recurring operating system.

The master brand should remain broader than the first product. Revenue Intelligence OS is where the company earns the right to expand.

## Positioning

### Category
Decision / Revenue Intelligence for commercial teams.

### One-line promise
**Turn commercial signals into decisions, actions and measurable learning.**

### Current wedge
Real-estate sales teams with digital lead acquisition, multiple advisors and a CRM/reporting workflow that still leaves management questions unresolved.

### We are not
- another generic dashboard;
- an ERP replacement;
- an “AI agency” selling automation before understanding the workflow;
- a black-box lead score;
- a mass-outreach tool.

## Mission

**Convertir datos comerciales dispersos en decisiones ejecutables y medibles.**

## Vision

**Que cualquier equipo pueda saber qué hacer hoy, por qué y si funcionó.**

## Brand belief

**Data is not the destination. A better decision is.**

## Operating narrative

`Observe → Diagnose → Decide → Act → Learn`

Every product story should make this loop visible.

## Personality

The brand should feel:
- precise;
- calm;
- rigorous;
- curious;
- operational;
- candid;
- premium without luxury theater;
- technical without jargon performance.

Avoid:
- hype;
- “revolutionary AI” language;
- fake urgency;
- guarantees without evidence;
- decorative metrics;
- pretending automation is intelligence.

## Voice

Prefer:
> “Detectamos dónde se rompe el funnel y qué acción merece prioridad.”

Avoid:
> “Nuestra IA revolucionaria optimiza 360° tu ecosistema comercial.”

Prefer:
> “Primero medimos el baseline. Después decidimos si automatizar.”

Avoid:
> “Automatizamos todo tu negocio con agentes.”

## Visual system

### Core colors
- **Warm canvas:** `#F3F0E8`
- **Ink:** `#11130F`
- **Dark surface:** `#121511`
- **Acid signal:** `#A7FF4F`
- **Muted text:** `#686B61`
- **Border:** `#D9D6CC`
- **Warning amber:** `#F2C15D`
- **Risk red:** `#FF7A68`

### Color meaning
Acid green is a **signal**, not decoration. Use for:
- current focus;
- positive state;
- key underline/highlight;
- live/active indicator.

Red/amber communicate operational risk, never brand personality.

### Typography
Current web stack:
`Inter, ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif`

Large headlines use tight tracking. Body copy should remain highly readable.

### Shape language
- medium-radius cards;
- strong rectangular information hierarchy;
- subtle shadows;
- pills reserved for state/metadata;
- dark control-room surfaces for evidence/diagnostic moments.

## Logo / mark

Current provisional mark: **VA** in a dark square with acid signal.

Do not create multiple unrelated logos while the brand thesis is still being validated.

## Founder avatar

Until a real approved portrait exists, use the **DD monogram placeholder**.

When a portrait is supplied:
- neutral/premium treatment;
- recognizable likeness;
- avoid cartoon exaggeration unless explicitly chosen;
- use the same crop consistently in concierge, founder section and social preview;
- keep a monogram fallback for loading/accessibility.

## Conversational concierge

The concierge is part of the brand.

It must say what it is:
> “Soy el concierge interactivo de Venture Architect. Te ayudo a orientar el problema y, si tiene sentido, preparo el contexto para que Diego lo revise.”

Never imply:
- a live human is typing when they are not;
- a model has inspected customer data it has not received;
- guaranteed business outcomes.

## Public trust content

A visitor should be able to discover:
1. what we solve;
2. how the product works;
3. who is behind it;
4. mission / vision / principles;
5. how data is handled;
6. what AI is and is not doing;
7. pilot scope and price hypothesis;
8. synthetic demo;
9. how to request a diagnostic.

## CTA hierarchy

### Primary
**Diagnosticar mi funnel**

### Secondary
**Ver control room**

### Exploratory
**Conocer Venture Architect**

### Human handoff
**Hablar con Diego**

The site should never present more than one visually dominant CTA in the same decision moment.

## Brand test

Before publishing a page or message ask:
- Does it explain a decision/problem, or merely sound sophisticated?
- Is every claim demonstrable?
- Is the next action obvious?
- Would a commercial manager understand it in under 15 seconds?
- Does it preserve trust if the visitor is skeptical?
