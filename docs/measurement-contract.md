# Measurement Contract

## North-star for Phase 0

**External demand evidence by Day 8.**

This is not MAU, code shipped, or demo polish.

## Funnel definitions

### Prospect added
A named target account with a plausible buyer/contact route.

### Outbound sent
A personalized first contact delivered to a target buyer/champion. Bulk generic spam does not count.

### Reply
A human response of any kind.

### Positive reply
Interest in discussing the problem, seeing the diagnostic/demo or asking a relevant question.

### Qualified conversation
A live or asynchronous exchange confirming a current problem, plausible buyer/process and next step.

### Meeting booked
A calendar commitment with a prospect/champion/decision maker for discovery, demo or pilot discussion.

### Lead inbound
A person/company voluntarily submits the MVP form or directly contacts the founder because of the offer.

### Proposal
A concrete commercial scope and price is sent.

### Won
A paid pilot or recurring contract.

## Day-8 gate

Minimum continue:
- >=3 qualified positive conversations / inbound leads,
- and >=1 meeting or proposal request.

Strong continue:
- >=5 qualified conversations,
- >=2 meetings,
- or >=1 paid pilot.

Pivot trigger:
- >=80 well-targeted contacts with <3% positive response,
- >=10 conversations but pain does not repeat,
- or repeated interest but no urgency/approval path.

## Leading operating ratios

- Reply rate = replies / outbound sent.
- Qualified-conversation rate = qualified conversations / replies.
- Meeting rate = meetings / qualified conversations.
- Visitor-to-lead = leads / unique-ish page sessions (MVP uses page views as approximate denominator).
- Sell/build ratio = selling hours / build hours.

## Founder time rule

During the 7-day validation window:

> Selling + customer-learning time should normally exceed build time.

Exception: a live qualified opportunity is blocked by one small, testable missing capability.
